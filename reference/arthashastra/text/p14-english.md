14.03.81 | Vessels of food and drink placed in those are not exhausted.
14.03.82 | When the night spectacle has begun, having cut out the teats of a dead cow, he should have [them] burnt in the lamp-fires.
14.03.83 | Having had the burnt [teats] ground with the urine of a bull, he should have a new pot smeared inside.
14.03.84 | Having led [it] around that village in the reverse direction, whatever fresh butter of these has been placed there, all that comes.

14.04.1 | Remedy, in one's own party, against the tainting poisons and garas employed by the enemy.
14.04.2 | Washing water mixed with a decoction of śleṣmātaka, kapittha, danti, danta, śaṭha, goji, śirīṣa, pāṭalī, balā, syonāga, punarnavā, śveta and vāraṇa, and mixed with candana, sālā, vṛkī and lohita: [it is] for those to be used by the king, a washing of the private parts for women, and a remedy against poison for the army.
14.04.3 | Powder of mahīrājī mixed with the bile of the spotted deer, mongoose, peacock and godhā, [and] the compound of sindu, vārita, varaṇa, vāruṇī, taṇḍulīyaka, the tip of śataparva and piṇḍītaka, [is] the remover of the ill effect of madana.
14.04.4 | A drink, mixed with milk, of any one or of all together of the decoctions of the roots of sṛgālavinnā, madana, sindu, vārita, varaṇa and vāraṇavallī removes the ill effect of madana.
14.04.5 | Sesame oil [prepared with] kaiḍarya and pūti removes madness; [it is] an action through the nose.
14.04.6 | A compound of priyaṅgu and naktamāla removes kuṣṭha.
14.04.7 | A compound of kuṣṭha and lodhra destroys pāka and śoṣa.
14.04.8 | Powder of kaṭaphala, dravantī and vilaṅga, an action through the nose, removes head-disease.
14.04.9 | A compound of priyaṅgu, mañjiṣṭhāta, garalā, kṣāra, samadhuka, haridrā and kṣaudra [is] for bringing back again those made unconscious by rope, water, poison, blow or fall.
14.04.10 | For men, the measure of an akṣa; for cattle and horses, twofold; fourfold for elephants and camels.
14.04.11 | And a jewel of these, having gold within, removes all poison.
14.04.12 | The jewel of jīvantī, śvetā, muṣkaka, puṣpa and vandāka, [and] of the aśvattha born on an akṣīva, removes all poison.
