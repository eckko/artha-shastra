- (5.5) svāmin/bhartṛ in the courtier's relation = master (स्वामी); mitra in the personal sense (courtier, friends) = friend (मित्र), not the prakṛti 'ally'; upakṛṣṭa = drawn near (doubtful)
- (5.5) iṅgita = hint (संकेत); ākāra = outward appearance (आकृति); tuṣṭa-/atuṣṭa-jñāna = knowledge of the satisfied/dissatisfied; '— iti tuṣṭa-jñānam' = — such is the knowledge of the satisfied (— यह सन्तुष्ट का ज्ञान है)
- (5.5) udaya = revenue (आय); vyaya = expenditure (व्यय); vyavasāya = resolve (निश्चय), never Hindi व्यवसाय; amānuṣa = non-human (अमनुष्य); asthāna = out of place (अनुचित स्थान); vyasana of the master in 5.5 unit 4 = vice, of the courtier (unit 7) = calamity
- (5.5) teachers: Kātyāyana = Katyayana (कात्यायन); Kaṇiṅka Bhāradvāja = Kaninka Bharadvaja (कणिङ्क भारद्वाज); Dīrgha Cārāyaṇa = Dirgha Charayana (दीर्घ चारायण); Ghoṭamukha = Ghotamukha (घोटमुख); Kiñjalka = Kinjalka (किञ्जल्क); Piśuna = Pishuna (पिशुन); 'iti X pravavrāja' = "...," [said] X, and went forth (ऐसा कहकर X निकल गए)
