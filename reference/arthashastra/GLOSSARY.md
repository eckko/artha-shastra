- (1.9) avagraha (a man's quality, su-avagraha, unit 1 and 3) = restraint (संयम), su-avagraha = of good restraint (उत्तम संयम वाला); kṛta-śilpa = with skill made (किए हुए शिल्प वाला), 'acquired/trained' only in notes; abhijana = high birth (कुल), as 5.6
- (1.9) pratyakṣa / parokṣa / anumeya = direct / indirect / to be inferred (प्रत्यक्ष / परोक्ष / अनुमेय); rāja-vṛtti = the king's conduct (राजा की वृत्ति); a verbless definition ('svayaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ pratyakṣam') = "Seen by oneself [is] direct", Hindi without है; -tva abstracts keep the suffix in Hindi (वाग्मित्व, सम्प्रियत्व, अवैरत्व), as 1.7
- (1.9) udita-udita = risen-risen (उदित-उदित), literal, 'exalted' only in notes; sa-aṅga = with limbs (अङ्गों सहित); daive nimitte = in the divine omen (दैव निमित्त में), read as one phrase, doubt in notes; atharvabhir upāyaiś ca = by the Atharvans and by means (अथर्वों और उपायों द्वारा), low-to-medium, 'Atharvan means' in notes only; pratikartṛ = counteracter (प्रतिकार करने वाला)
- (1.9) kathā-yoga = occasions of conversation (वार्तालाप के प्रसंग), literal 'connections of talk' in notes; pratibhānavattva stays pratibhānavattva (प्रतिभानवत्त्व), 'ready wit' in English brackets only; akṛta-avekṣaṇa = viewing of the not done (न किए हुए का अवलोकन); deśa-kāla-atyaya = lapse of place and time (देश और काल का अत्यय), atyaya kept in Hindi
